Can the Broncos still make the eight?

Extinct in all but paperwork · ceiling now below six teams' banked totals

Step 1 — the ceiling. Brisbane has 18 points with 5 games left and no byes remaining. Two points per win:

18 + (5 × 2) = 28 points maximum ← requires winning all five

Step 2 — the wall. Last week four teams were out of range. After Round 22 it is six — every one of them is guaranteed to finish on 30 or more, above anything Brisbane can reach:

Panthers 36 → beyond reach Roosters 34 → beyond reach Warriors 32 → beyond reach Sharks 30 → beyond reach outright Dolphins 30 → beyond reach outright Knights 28 + bye = 30 → beyond reach outright

Eighth place allows seven teams above you. Six slots are already spoken for, so exactly one of the remaining ten sides may finish ahead of Brisbane. Every other contender has to fall.

Step 3 — what "mathematically alive" now requires. Each rival's floor is its points plus 2 per bye remaining; the last column is the most they can win and still finish at or below Brisbane's 28:

Step 4 — the verdict. Strictly, the formal certificate has not been issued: formal elimination needs eight teams guaranteed beyond reach on points, and the count sits at six, with the Cowboys' floor of 28 merely equal to Brisbane's ceiling. But look at what the surviving scenario demands, all at once:

Brisbane win 5/5 → 28 pts Cowboys win 0 of 4 (+bye) → 28 pts, AND Brisbane claw back a 58-point differential gap (−98 v −156) Rabbitohs and Bulldogs (bar one) → essentially stop winning: at 28 apiece their differentials (+78, −44) still beat anything Brisbane can build from −156

Put plainly: the perfect scenario — five wins from five for a side that has won six games all year — still only reaches 28, which six teams have already banked more than. Best case, Brisbane finish 7th in fantasy-land and 8th requires roughly fifteen specific results elsewhere. The formal paperwork arrives with almost any two of: another Brisbane loss, a Rabbitohs win, a Bulldogs win — and Souths play the Sharks today, so the countdown is measured in hours, not weeks. Last week the required miracle was "six teams collapse"; this week it is "six teams collapse and the laws of differential bend". That is the polite mathematical phrasing of over.

How the wall was built in one round: Brisbane's loss froze them at 18 while the Knights (win) and Dolphins (win) both cleared 30-or-guaranteed, converting last week's "0 of 5 / 0 of 6" long shots into outright impossibilities. The Round 21 footnote — that the first loss from here effectively ends it — has now happened.

Looking to 2026/27

  • The Championship expands its play-offs from four teams to six this season — a meaningfully wider door for a club that finished 17th.
  • A new club crest debuts, reviving the 1977–92 badge: bottle kiln, Staffordshire knot, red-and-white stripes.
  • Stuart Harvey appointed technical director in May, joining sporting director Jon Walters in a restructured recruitment setup.
  • Mark Robins stays on for his second full season, backed by owner John Coates with real money — Stoke have been among the Championship's bigger spenders this window.
  • For Brisbane viewing: a 3pm Saturday kick-off in Stoke lands around midnight AEST.
