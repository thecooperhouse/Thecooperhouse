The season is gone — but the spoon just got buried

Eliminated 8 AugSpoon arithmetically impossible

Step 1 — the ceiling, unchanged in verdict. Brisbane have 20 points, two games left, no byes:

20 + (2 × 2) = 24 points maximum ← requires winning out

Eighth place (Cowboys) sits on 30, a floor since points can't go backwards. 24 < 30 — still eliminated, exactly as it has been since 8 August.

Step 2 — the one that actually changed this week. Last week's page had the wooden spoon as "mathematically live." The Raiders win put it to bed:

Broncos 20 pts, 2 games, 0 byes → FLOOR = 20 (can't lose points) Dragons 14 pts, 2 games, 0 byes → CEILING = 14 + (2 × 2) = 18 Dragons ceiling (18) < Broncos floor (20) → Dragons cannot mathematically finish above Brisbane → Brisbane cannot finish last, regardless of result

The Dragons simply don't have enough games left to catch a Brisbane side that can no longer lose points. That single Round 25 win, worth nothing on the ladder, quietly closed off the one embarrassment still available to this season.

Dragons' ceiling exactly equals Titans' floor — so the Dragons can only avoid finishing bottom by winning both remaining games and the Titans losing all three of theirs, which would then come down to points differential (Dragons −293 vs Titans −154 today). Brisbane are spectators to this one now.

Step 3 — who's still alive for eighth, verified. Cowboys' 30-point floor is the bar. Below that line, only two clubs clear a ceiling of 30 or better:

Bulldogs 28 + (2 × 2) = 32 ← alive outright · play Brisbane, Rd 27 Sea Eagles 26 + (2 × 2) = 30 ← alive, exactly on the bar Storm 24 + (2 × 2) = 28 ← ELIMINATED (was alive last week) Raiders 24 + (1 × 2) = 26 ← ELIMINATED (was alive last week)

Two of last week's four hopefuls are gone. Brisbane's remaining fixtures now split cleanly: Round 26 against an eliminated Storm side means nothing to anyone, Round 27 against a Bulldogs side that's still fighting means Brisbane get one last chance to be an irritant.

The honest summary. Nothing about the ladder position changed for the better this week — 14th is still 14th of 17, a long way from anyone's idea of a title defence. What changed is smaller but real: a genuinely good comeback win, the wooden spoon off the table, and Round 27 against Canterbury turning into a game with some actual jeopardy for someone else. Small mercies, taken where available.
