How to read this dashboard

Gold timing chips show the winner’s race time; other chips show finishing gaps. ≈ means approximate. F1 circuit records are race lap records; MotoGP circuit records may come from qualifying. Unverified timings stay blank. The latest weekend, current standings, title analysis and news remain hidden until you tick its watched box. Earlier races stay visible.

Title estimates are inherited from the previous edition, not newly calculated forecasts. Maximum points and eligibility are calculated from the standings and remaining calendar, assuming full points and that every listed round goes ahead. Calendar changes can alter those figures.

Watched choices are saved in this browser when storage is available. Untick the box to hide results again.
