How to read this dashboard

Gold chips show the winner’s session time; other chips show the published gap. MotoGP GP race lap records are separate from all-time records and Sprint laps. Blank or omitted values are not verified. The one watched box for each series reveals its latest GP and Sprint, standings, championship analysis and news. Earlier results remain accessible.

Watched choices are saved for the specific event in this browser when storage is available. Untick to hide results again. A newly completed event starts protected. Archives keep their original behaviour.

Probabilities were recalculated on 5 October 2026 using 200,000 simulations per championship. They are experimental estimates with limited historical validation. Maximum possible points and mathematical elimination are calculated separately; zero simulated titles do not mean elimination.
