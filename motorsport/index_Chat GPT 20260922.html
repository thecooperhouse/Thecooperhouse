How to read this dashboard

Gold timing chips show the winner’s race time; other chips show finishing gaps. ≈ means approximate. F1 circuit records are race lap records; MotoGP circuit records may come from qualifying. Records and race notes stay hidden until both sessions of that weekend are marked watched.

Title estimates are illustrative values retained from the original dashboard. Its simulation code was not supplied, so the percentages cannot be reproduced here. Arithmetic is calculated from the saved standings and scheduled remaining rounds, assuming full points and no calendar changes.

Watched choices are saved in this browser when storage is available. Every race starts hidden in a fresh browser. External source pages can contain spoilers.
