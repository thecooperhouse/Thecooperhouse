Snapshot: 28 September 2026. This is a saved dashboard, not a live feed. Round 15 of the F1 season (Azerbaijan, 24–26 September) was added on 28 September against the official formula1.com classification, cross-checked against The Race, RacingNews365, grandprix.com and Sky Sports. MotoGP has not raced since the Red Bull Ring on 20 September, so its results, standings and title panel are unchanged from the 21 September edition; only its news feed and dates have moved. Earlier rounds and their historical timing data are retained from previous editions, and not every timing figure or news quotation in them has been independently verified.

Where sources disagree. Three conflicts in this edition, none of them resolved by picking quietly. (1) Baku's fastest lap. grandprix.com and Total-Motorsport both credit George Russell, 1:44.916 on lap 49; formula1.com's own race report says in passing that Verstappen set it. Two specific, agreeing sources beat one unspecific one, so Russell's name is in the card — but it is flagged there too. (2) Antonelli's finishing gap. The official classification has him +14.512s in fifth; The Race printed +15.512s. Neither figure appears on the page, since only the podium carries chips. (3) Constructors' points. Yahoo Sports published Red Bull Racing on 249 and Racing Bulls on 96; formula1.com has 263 and 83. Only the official pair reconciles with the 1,695 points actually awarded, so that pair is used. Carried over from earlier editions: no official winner's race time was ever published for the MotoGP British GP, and one outlet's fastest lap for the MotoGP Austrian GP is uncorroborated, so both chips stay null.

How to read this dashboard

Gold timing chips show the winner’s race time; other chips show finishing gaps. ≈ means approximate. F1 circuit records are race lap records; MotoGP circuit records may come from qualifying. The latest weekend’s results, records and notes stay hidden until its watched box is ticked. Earlier rounds are always visible.

Title estimates are produced here, not inherited: 200,000 simulated remainders per championship, re-run on 28 September 2026 from the standings on this page (seed 20260928). The model, its fitting method and its known weaknesses are described under each title table. The elimination lines, maximum totals and earliest-clinch rounds are plain arithmetic from those standings and the scheduled remaining rounds, assuming full points and no calendar changes — which, with Qatar and Abu Dhabi still undecided in F1 and Qatar still unconfirmed in MotoGP, is an assumption rather than a fact.

Watched choices are saved in this browser when storage is available. Only the latest weekend starts hidden in a fresh browser. External source pages can contain spoilers.
